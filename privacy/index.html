Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the website codesarray.com, published by Codesarray. The Android games have their own policies, linked at the end.

What the site collects itself

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no comments, no forms and no analytics of its own. It is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps ordinary server logs (your IP address and the pages requested) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Stored in your browser

If you switch between the dark and light theme, your choice is kept in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it. It is one word, it never leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it.

Fonts

The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts (Google LLC). When they load, your browser sends Google the request, which includes your IP address. Google's use of that data is described in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Advertising

The site shows ads through Google AdSense (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology, and may collect your IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device information, the pages you visit on this site and your interaction with the ads. Where you have consented, this data is used to show ads based on your interests and your visits to this and other sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where Google's consent messages apply, you are asked for consent before any personalised ad is shown. You can change your choice at any time from the consent message's own link.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com, and from many other ad companies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works fully without them.

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Links to other sites

Articles link to the videos on YouTube and to other sites. Those sites have their own policies; YouTube's is the Google Privacy Policy. Nothing from YouTube is embedded on these pages, so YouTube sees nothing until you follow a link.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and personalised ads are only shown where consent has been given.

The games

The Android games have separate policies: Blockfall, Paws & Perils and Synapsy.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date revised first.
