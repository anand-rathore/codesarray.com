# codesarray.com

The Codesarray website: a static site on GitHub Pages, published the moment
`main` is pushed. `README.md` has the full layout and design notes;
`brand\README.md` has the identity. This file is the short list of rules.

## Addresses never move

Google Play, AdMob and Search Console point at this site. Restyle existing
pages, but never move, rename or delete them. The protected addresses are
listed under "URLs that must never change" in `README.md`. New content gets
new addresses. Before every push, `python tools\check-links.py` must print
`ok`.

## Articles keep one structure

Articles are filed by language (the category), then by level (the
sub-category), matching the video tracks:

```
articles\categories.json                         languages and levels, in display order
articles\<language>\<level>\<slug>\article.md    one article (source of truth)
```

- Address: `/articles/<language>/<level>/<slug>/`. Permanent once pushed; if
  an article ever has to move, add the old address to its `aliases:` line.
- Languages use the same folder names as the video project (`python`,
  `javascript`, `java`, ...). Levels are `beginner`, `intermediate`,
  `advanced` and are shared by every language. Do not add other levels or
  sub-categories without asking the user.
- A long-form video's article gets `lesson: <its number>`. A short's article
  goes in the level of the video it promotes and gets `follows: <that number>`.
- The slug does not repeat the language or the level.
- Never edit a generated `index.html` under `articles\` by hand. Edit the
  `article.md` or `build-articles.py`, then run `python build-articles.py`.
- The three newest articles are also listed by hand in the `#articles`
  section of the front page.

The full rules for placing and writing an article, and the order of work
that leads to one, are in `G:\youtube\CLAUDE.md` ("Articles" and "Order of
work for every new video or short").

## Other rules

- The site speaks as Codesarray. The owner's name appears only in the work
  section and on `/work/`, worded "Codesarray is created by ...". No
  employer names, phone number or personal email anywhere.
- The Videos section on the front page lists long-form videos only, each
  linked to its own YouTube URL. Shorts are not listed there.
- One identity on every page: the home page, work, articles, the game
  pages and the privacy policies all use the Trace identity. No page has
  its own palette.
- Every page has a dark and a light theme with a switch in the header.
  Style with the colour tokens only (`--bg`, `--text`, `--text-dim`,
  `--border`, `--gold`, `--brand`), never a hard-coded colour, and check
  every change in both themes. The light tokens appear twice in
  `styles.css`; keep the two blocks identical. Every page needs the
  one-line `data-mode` script and `theme.js` in its head and the
  `.mode-toggle` button in its header nav.
- The privacy policies are generated: change the header or head in
  `build-privacy.py`, never in the `.html`, and never change their wording
  here (it is copied from each game's own repository).
- Brand assets are generated by `python brand\make_brand.py`; do not edit
  the PNG or SVG files by hand.
