Blockfall Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

Blockfall (“the app”) is published by Codesarray. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect ourselves

None, apart from the optional Google Play Games sign-in described below: Blockfall has no accounts, no analytics of its own, and the game itself sends nothing to Codesarray.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your current games, best scores, statistics, and preferences on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Advertising

Blockfall shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). Some ads are optional rewarded ads you choose to watch; a small banner appears on the game screen and an ad may appear occasionally between games.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its partners: your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This data is used for advertising and ad measurement, and for personalised ads only where you have consented.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where Google's consent messages apply, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy options” on the home screen.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information from the same “Privacy options” entry.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google shows your Play Games profile (player name and avatar) to us and to other players on the daily leaderboard, and we send your daily scores to Google so the leaderboard can display them. Sign-in is optional: the game works fully without it. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Permissions

The app uses Internet access for ads only. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

Blockfall is not directed at children under 13, and personalised ads are only shown where consent has been given.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email codesarray@gmail.com, the same address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
