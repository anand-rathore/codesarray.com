Four games, one workout

Synapsy is four classic memory games in one: match the pairs, repeat the sequence, recall the grid, and spot what changed. Play them through a 120-level campaign, or take the Daily Workout: three short rounds, the same puzzle for everyone, about three minutes in all.

Synapsy

Android · Free · Plays offline · Optional Remove Ads

  • Four ways to remember

    Pairs is the classic memory match: flip two cards, find every pair. Sequence lights up coloured pads in order, then you repeat it. Grid Recall flashes a pattern, then you tap the cells you saw. What Changed shows a tray of icons, blinks, and asks what is different. Later worlds add a gentle timer, decoys and cards that move.

  • A daily habit with forgiving streaks

    Finish the Daily Workout to grow your streak. Miss a day and a streak freeze covers you; you earn one every seven days, and a missed day can be repaired too. No ads and no hints inside the workout, and everyone gets the same puzzle, so the leaderboard is fair.

  • Made to be comfortable

    Large cards, clear shapes and high contrast, and colour is never the only clue. A dark Dusk theme for the evening and a light Dawn theme for the day. It works with large system fonts, and sound, vibration and a Reduce motion switch are all yours to set. Retry any level as often as you like: no lives, no energy, no waiting.

Fair with ads

No banner ads. No ad before your first game, and never an ad around the Daily Workout. An occasional full-screen ad between campaign levels, a few minutes apart at most, plus optional rewarded ads for an extra hint or to repair a missed streak day.

Remove Ads

One purchase takes the full-screen ads away for good and adds more daily hints, free streak repair and three extra card backs. Sign in to Google Play Games (optional) for cloud save, achievements and leaderboards.
